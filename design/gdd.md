## 1. Player Promise

**One-line promise.**

You are a rustler in a saucer. You beam cows and fire them into a shared black hole that grows on every cow fed.

**One familiar comparison.**

A three-minute flying score attack, then a shared carnival cannon. The prize is not your score. It is a black hole the whole World is feeding, and a radio show that only gets new episodes when the hole has eaten enough.

**Why this game.**

This world started as a night exhibit for our own alien collection and the song that goes with it. The cow game was the part people actually stayed for, so the rest of the place grew out of that: a pen, a cannon, a hole that remembers every cow, and two farmers on the radio telling you what the hole is doing. We want the exhibit to be a place you come back to, not a video you watch once.

## 2. First Minutes & How to Play

| Time | Player experience |
|---|---|
| **0–5 seconds after control** | You spawn at night in front of a video dome. A pink PLAY sign bobs over a pod. |
| **5–10 seconds** | You walk into the pod. It seals and lifts you. You are going up to catch cows. |
| **10–60 seconds** | You are the saucer. You press, a beam drops, a cow rises, and the catch counter moves. |
| **1–3 minutes** | You leave the pasture. Your cows stand in the cargo pen. You pick one up and it sits in your hand. |
| **3–10 minutes** | You carry it to the cannon and fire. It flies out into the star ring. You are feeding the hole. |
| **Natural stopping point** | The hole passes and pays you. The radio by the door shows the next episode and how many cows it still needs. |

**Player-facing How to Play.**

- Step into the pod, then beam cows
- Carry one cow over to the cannon
- Everyone shares the pen and the hole

## 3. Core Loop

| # | Step (verb) | What the player does (Player input → what they see or hear → what changes) | Why do it again? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beam | Fly the saucer and drop the beam → a cow lifts, the counter ticks, combo climbs | The next cow is already running, and the combo is still live. |
| 2 | Carry | Click a cow in the pen → it attaches to your hand, and everyone else sees it | The pen is shared, so a cow you don't take is a cow someone else fires. |
| 3 | Fire | Click the cannon → it loads, charges, recoils, and the cow arcs into the ring | You can see your shot, and so can anyone standing there. |
| 4 | Feed | Wait for the hole, or watch a raider take a cow → the cow vanishes and kudos land | The hole is on a two-minute pass, and the whole ring goes in together. |

| | |
|---|---|
| **One complete loop takes** | A catch is about 2 seconds. Catch, carry and fire is about 2 minutes, and it ends when your cow is in the ring. The payoff before the hole pays is the shot itself. A pasture round is 3 minutes. |
| **Decision, challenge, or expression** | Where to drop the beam, when to leave a round early (caught cows are kept), and whether to fire a cow now or leave it for someone else. |
| **Shortest satisfying visit / typical session** | 4 / 12 minutes. One cow fired is enough. A typical visit is one round plus a few shots. |
| **Why repetition 10 remains appealing** | The pasture does not change, but the place does: the hole is larger, the sky has shifted, debris is up, and the radio has a new episode. Other people's cows are in the pen when you arrive. |

**Pillars.**

1. The catch is the toy — aiming the beam is the skill, and it has to stay fun alone.
2. One herd — the pen is everyone's, and a carried cow is visible on your body.
3. The hole remembers — every cow anyone feeds is still in the World tomorrow.

## 4. Why Players Come Back

### 4.1 The next-day (D1) sentence

> "A player who enjoyed their first session returns the next day (D1) because the collection hook, the next radio episode, is still locked behind a cow count they can read on the radio, which they saw before they left."

### 4.2 The progression chain

> **Repeatable action → persistent reward → new capability, status, or social role → new decision or challenge**

| Moment | What persists or has been built? | What becomes possible next? | How can another player tell? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **End of first session** | Cows in the shared pen, a board score, and the first radio episode | The radio names the next episode and the cows it still needs | Your cow is in the pen or in their hand; your name is on the board |
| **End of first week** | A higher cosmos tier: a bigger hole, a shifted sky, maybe a second ship | New episodes, and the hole is a different object than the one you met | The hole and the sky are the same for everyone in the World |
| **Week 3+ — what takes more than two weeks?** | The upper tiers (600 cows and beyond) and the last episodes. One player cannot rush this. | The World looks occupied: debris, a second ship, a finished show | A newcomer can see the grown hole without being told |

You come back and the hole is bigger than you left it, because other people fed it. The radio is already on a later episode. You fly one round, fire what you catch, and the sign still shows a number you have not reached.

Kudos: 10 per cow, paid to the player who fired it when the hole or a raider takes it. Nothing spends them yet. The abuse risk is fake round scores; the server drops any round that is not a plausible catch count.

### 4.3 Two return hooks

| Selected hook | Exact trigger or timing | What the player anticipates | Reminder channel + no-reminder fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Collection — the radio and the hole** | Six episodes unlock at 0, 10, 30, 75, 150 and 300 cows fed by everyone. The radio shows the next threshold. Live now. | A new episode, and a hole that is visibly bigger | No push reminder. Fallback: the radio label is the last thing by the door |
| **2. Weekly leaderboard reset** | The pasture board resets Monday 00:00 UTC. Not built yet. This is the V0 slice. | A board you can still reach, instead of a permanent best | No push reminder. Fallback: the board in the dome shows the reset and your last score |

## 5. Social by Design

| | |
|---|---|
| **The repeatable social loop** | Player A catches cows into the shared pen → Player B picks one up and walks it to the cannon → they fire it, and everyone sees the shot → the hole pays the shooter and grows for the whole World, which is the reason to catch another. |
| **The disappearance test** | The loop does not break. You can catch, carry and fire alone, and the hole still pays you. What you lose is a full pen you didn't stock, visible shots that aren't yours, and a hole that grows while you are away. |
| **From strangers to a group** | The pen and the cannon are the two meeting points, both labelled in the world. A newcomer copies whoever is carrying a cow. No voice and no shared language. |
| **Recognition & continuity** | Names are on the pasture board. A carried cow stays on your avatar until you fire or it wanders back (3 minutes). The hole and the episodes are a shared memory: everyone is looking at the same grown world. |
| **Quiet hours & player counts** | Alone, you fly a round, fire your cows, and listen to whatever episode the hole has unlocked. If nobody has fed it yet, episode one is already there and two free cows appear when the pen is empty. Social play starts at 2. A good group is 4 to 8. Target is 20. Not tested at that count. A solo player meets others at the pen, because that is where caught cows wait. |
| **Drop-in / drop-out** | A late arrival can fire a cow from the pen without flying a round. If someone leaves mid-carry, the cow returns to the pen after 3 minutes. Their departure does not stop anyone else's round or shot. |
| **Visible play (the bystander test)** | Someone walks past with a cow under their arm, the cannon lifts and shakes, and the cow flies out through a portal. |
| **Shareable play (the memorable moment)** | The ring of cows folding into the black hole while the cannon is still smoking. |
| **Bring-a-friend** | The moment the pen is empty, you want someone up in the pasture catching. Once they arrive, the pen fills while you fire, and the hole pass is a thing you watched together. |

## 6. Mobile-First

**Every core-loop verb on touch.**

| Core-loop verb | How it works with touch controls |
|---|---|
| Beam | Tap the field to drop the beam. Move with the stick. |
| Carry | Tap a cow in the pen. It attaches to your hand. |
| Fire | Tap the cannon. The shot plays on its own. |
| Feed | No input. The hole passes on its own; you watch. |

**UI plan.**

Almost no HUD for the loop. The pink PLAY sign, the pen label and the cannon are objects in the world, big enough to read on a phone. The catch counter is one line at the bottom of the screen.

**Performance.**

Target: 60fps on recommended desktop hardware and 30fps on a **Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8** (the developer's phone) in the mobile client, at 20 players. Not profiled yet.

**Desktop-only dependencies.**

The pasture forces first person, and the dome is a video. If either is missing on mobile, the pasture falls back to third person and the dome keeps playing picture without blocking the loop. The four verbs do not need the video.

## 7. World, Look & Story

**Story / world.**

A night field under a video dome, where a saucer is stealing cows and firing them into a hole that is getting too big. Two farmers, Dale and Tammy, narrate it from a radio by the door, one episode each time the hole eats enough.

**Visual direction.**

Permanent night, one pink beam, one green pen, one purple hole. Those three colors are the whole interface: beam means catch, green means the cow is yours to carry, purple means the prize. The signature shot is the cannon firing a cow through a pink portal into a ring of floating cows, with the hole behind them. Players keep their own avatars. The cow in their hand is ours.

## 8. Audience & Comparables

**Primary player + arrival context.**

For players who already enjoy short score-attack party games and strange night worlds, arriving alone from Discover, looking for a few minutes of flying and a place that is bigger when they come back.

**How the first group arrives.**

The first groups come from the Knights of Antrom community and from Discover, because the world is already public and the other Antrom worlds link here. Two people are enough for the pen to matter, so a pair arriving together already clears the social threshold.

**Deliberately not for.**

Players who want a fair competitive reward. The hole pays the person who fires the cow, not the person who caught it.

### Comparables

| | Comparable A — outside Decentraland: Katamari Damacy | Comparable B — outside Decentraland: a fairground high-striker |
|---|---|---|
| What we observed works | Feeding one thing until it is visibly bigger is the whole pleasure, and the growth is the score | A three-minute skill toy, a public result, and a crowd that watches the hit |
| What does not fit our audience or context—and why | It is solo, and when the authored levels end there is nothing left to feed | The prize is yours alone, so a friend watching cannot change the outcome |
| What we will do differently | The thing being fed is shared, persists overnight, and unlocks a story instead of a new level | The skill toy stocks a pen anyone can fire, so the crowd is playing, not watching |

## 9. 4 Week Plan (v1 scope)

This application is for V0. The loop above is already live at `abducted.dcl.eth`. These four weeks are the funded slice: the return reason, not a new game.

| Week | What is playable / done |
|---|---|
| 1 — Prototype definition | Daily target on the radio: one number (cows to fire) that resets at 00:00 UTC, visible before you leave. Mobile device named and the four verbs tried on it. |
| 2 — Core interaction + first-group test | Weekly board reset, Monday 00:00 UTC, with last week's winner still shown. Playtest with 2 players, the social threshold. |
| 3 — Core systems refinement | Exit view shows tomorrow's target and the next episode's cow count. Fix whatever the playtest showed in the first minute. |
| 4 — Playable release, including testing and deployment | Both hooks live in `abducted.dcl.eth`. One more playtest, then the build reviewers can open. |

**What keeps the experience changing after launch.**

- Without a new level, variation comes from who else fed the hole and which episode is on. The daily target changes every day. The board changes every week.
- If an update is skipped, other players' cows and the hole's next threshold still change the visit.
- Nothing resets your place in the story. A new player can catch and fire within 4 minutes, and the grown hole is a sight, not a wall.
- One player behaviour that would change what we build next: people firing cows they didn't catch, and catchers stopping because of it.

**Not building in v1.**

A new pasture set, more radio episodes, an emote-ownership gate, and paying the catcher as well as the shooter. If V0 hits its numbers, the catcher's cut comes back first in V1. Standing non-goal: never a second mini-game, because the beam is the toy.

**Top risk + fallback.**

People watch the video, fly one round, and never return. Fallback: the daily target and the next episode's cow count are the last thing they see, on the radio by the door, before they leave.
